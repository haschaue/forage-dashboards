This Week's Findings

WIN

Spend cut 25% and sales still grew 9.2% - the budget discipline worked across the whole company
This is the clearest cross-channel result we have had. Ad spend across the ten locations fell from $6,975 to $5,222, a cut of $1,753 or 25%, while net sales rose from $910,508 to $994,508, up 9.2% or roughly $84,000. ROAS improved at nine of the ten locations, and true ROAS - the number based on actual web purchases rather than proxy conversion actions - went from 1.24 to 1.42 company-wide. Sales rising while spend falls is the pattern that tells you the earlier budget was not buying the register; it was buying reported conversions. The trims recommended over the last several weeks have now been made and the sales base did not follow the money down. Action: hold this spend level rather than treating the sales growth as headroom to re-inflate budgets.

WIN

Champaign is up 43.6% with organic clicks up 53% - two weeks ago it was the worst store in the company
Champaign was the top concern three weeks ago, when sales and search were both falling together and it looked like genuine demand loss. It flipped positive last week at +16.0%, and it has now accelerated to +43.6% ($59,562 vs about $41,478). Organic clicks went from 238 to 365, a 53% jump, the largest search gain of any location. Both channels agree strongly upward, and spend was cut 27% to $299 over the same stretch, so none of this was bought. One thing to keep honest about it: Champaign's true ROAS is 0.94, still under break-even even after doubling from 0.47, and average search position sits at 6.4, the second-weakest in the company. The store is winning on demand, not on either paid or ranking strength. Action: find out from the operator what changed here, because it is the most transferable result on the board.

CONCERN

Pewaukee is the only location moving backwards on all three channels, with true ROAS now 0.90
Every other store improved somewhere this period. Pewaukee did not. Sales fell again, from -0.8% to -1.5% ($68,045), the worst in the company. Organic clicks were essentially flat at +1.6% (317 to 322) in a period where six locations posted double-digit search gains. And it is the only campaign whose ROAS declined at all, from 3.77 to 3.73, with true ROAS dropping from 1.25 to 0.90 - meaning the ads there now return less real revenue than they cost. It also carries the second-highest ad ratio at 0.87% of sales. Spend was already cut $228 this period and the numbers still went the wrong way, which rules out underfunding as the explanation. This has been flagged two weeks running and has worsened both times. Action: this is an operator visit, not a marketing adjustment - and the ad budget should come down toward the $460 Middleton benchmark until the store's own numbers turn.

WIN

MKE Public Market is up 20.4% on a 34% spend cut, the largest budget reduction anywhere
MKE was the number one concern a month ago when spend was climbing and sales were at -10.2%. The full reversal is now on the board: spend cut from $1,098 to $727, the biggest reduction of any location, and sales up 20.4% to $91,659 with organic clicks up 32% (248 to 328). Taking a third of the budget out and watching sales rise 20% is about as direct a proof as this data can offer that the spend was not driving the business. The caution that keeps this from being a clean win: MKE still runs the lowest ROAS in the company at 3.18, its true ROAS slipped slightly from 1.11 to 1.05, and at 0.79% of sales it is still the third-heaviest spender. Action: the trims are working, so keep going - there is likely another $150 to $200 to take out here before sales notice.

WATCH

Sun Prairie's search has now eroded three reads running, from 460 clicks to 374, while sales climb 15.7%
Sun Prairie keeps posting strong sales - up 15.7% to $125,652, second best in the company - but its organic channel has declined every time we have looked. Clicks went 460, then 431, now 374, and average position has drifted 2.2 to 2.6 to 2.9 across the same three reads. The decline is slowing (-24% last week, -13% this week) but it has not stopped, and steady position slippage alongside it points at a listings or ranking cause rather than anything happening in the restaurant. Separately, Whitefish Bay remains the mirror image and the best home for any freed-up budget: the strongest organic in the company at 627 clicks, a healthy 1.64 true ROAS, yet only 0.45% of sales in spend and sales still slightly negative at -0.7%. One data note for anyone acting on the spend figures: the ads pull covers 7/26 to 8/22 while sales and search run 8/1 to 8/28, so the windows are offset by about six days.
