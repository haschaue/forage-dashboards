ESCALATED - Champaign now slipping on branded queries too: page pos 6.0 -> 7.2 (4th straight period), 'forage kitchen champaign' 1.7 -> 3.0, menu variant 1.6 -> 4.0
This is the third week Champaign has been flagged and it has changed character. Clicks to /locations/champaign-il actually ROSE this period (262 -> 298, +14%), which on its own looks fine - but position kept drifting for a fourth consecutive period (4.4 -> 6.1 -> 6.8 -> 7.2) and the drop is now reaching the branded terms that were previously safe: 'forage kitchen champaign' fell from pos 1.7 to 3.0 (still +75 clicks on rising demand, masking the slide) and 'forage kitchen champaign menu' fell from 1.6 to 4.0 with clicks -14. Losing the #1 slot on your own store name is a ranking problem, not demand - the clicks are only holding because Champaign search volume is climbing into the UIUC fall term. If position stays at 3.0 when demand normalizes, the traffic goes with it. Action this week, in order: (1) run a URL inspection on /locations/champaign-il in Search Console - confirm it is indexed, canonical to itself, and last-crawled recently; this is the technical check flagged as the escalation trigger two weeks ago and it is now due. (2) Search 'forage kitchen champaign' from a Champaign IP and record who occupies positions 1-2 - if it is an aggregator (Yelp/DoorDash/Toast page) the fix is internal linking and page authority, not copy. (3) Verify the Champaign Google Business Profile is verified, category 'Restaurant', hours correct through the fall semester, with recent photos and steady review velocity. (4) Add campus/neighborhood terms (UIUC, Campustown, Green Street, downtown Champaign) to the page copy and confirm complete LocalBusiness schema.

/locations/sun-prairie fell 460 -> 350 clicks, the largest single-location loss this period and well past the -15% flag threshold, with impressions down 2,443 and position softening 2.2 -> 2.6. Most of this reads as demand rather than lost rankings: the core query 'forage kitchen sun prairie' held position exactly flat at 1.3 while losing 83 clicks, and the menu variant 'forage kitchen sun prairie menu' actually IMPROVED (pos 4.8 -> 3.9). Action: check the Sun Prairie Google Business Profile for anything that suppresses local visibility - hours changed for summer and never restored, a temporary-closure flag, missing/stale photos, or a review-velocity stall vs. the neighboring stores. Compare its GBP against Pewaukee's (which recovered and is now +34 clicks) and match whatever differs. Then confirm the page still carries full LocalBusiness schema with geo coordinates and a menu URL. Re-check next week: if position keeps sliding past 3.0 while clicks fall again, it is a ranking problem and should be treated like Champaign.

Also worth noting 'forage kitchen menu' itself lost 81 clicks even while improving to pos 1.2, so the generic term is fine and the geo-qualified ones are the casualties. Recovering these three to pos ~2 is worth roughly 60-80 clicks/28d plus whatever /menu regains.
