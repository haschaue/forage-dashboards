HIGH

Account True ROAS is ~1.0 — paid search roughly breaks even on tracked online orders
Across all stores, $7,296 of spend generated $7,283 in actual GA4 online-order revenue (True ROAS 1.00, down from 1.13 last period). On a food-margin basis, online orders alone do not cover the ad spend. The important caveat: in-store visits driven by these local campaigns (directions taps, calls, menu views) are NOT captured as revenue here, and for a dine-in/takeout business that in-store lift is likely the bigger return — so 1.0 is the measurable floor, not the full picture. Recommended next step to actually answer 'is this worth it': run a deliberate spend-reduction test in 2-3 markets for 4 weeks and watch whether same-store sales (Toast) drop — that isolates the incremental in-store value the online number can't see.

HIGH

Budget is concentrated on the LOWEST true-ROAS stores — Milwaukee & Champaign vs Whitefish Bay
Milwaukee is the top spender at $1,118 (+$233) yet has the second-worst True ROAS at 0.76 ($853 in orders). Champaign spends $414 at True ROAS 0.47 (worst; $194 in orders). Meanwhile Whitefish Bay — the BEST True ROAS at 1.58 — was cut $551 to just $567, even though it generated MORE online-order revenue ($895) than Milwaukee did on half the spend. Monona (1.32) is also strong and under-funded at $669. Shifting ~$300-500/period out of Milwaukee and Champaign into Whitefish Bay and Monona would raise measurable order revenue at equal spend. This misallocation shows up in blended ROAS too but is far starker on True ROAS.

HIGH

Sun Prairie True ROAS collapsed 1.67 -> 0.86; online orders fell from 52 to 17
Sun Prairie was the account's best online-order performer last period (True ROAS 1.67) and dropped to 0.86 — a -0.81 swing, by far the largest, on spend that was actually cut ($933 -> $562). Order revenue fell $1,557 -> $485. Blended ROAS barely moved (4.26 -> 3.97), which is exactly why this needs the True ROAS view to catch. Check Google Ads for an asset-group/audience-signal change around 06-22, and confirm the Sun Prairie online-ordering flow (Toast/GA4 purchase tag) is still firing — a tracking break would produce this exact pattern.

MEDIUM

Champaign has run below 0.50 True ROAS for two periods — chronically unprofitable on orders
Champaign's True ROAS is 0.47 (prev 0.45): $414 spend for $194 of online-order revenue (10 orders). Unlike stores that break even, this one is consistently the worst and is not improving. Two likely causes worth ruling out: (1) online ordering isn't well set up / promoted for the Champaign location, or (2) it's an out-of-market college store where the local-visit value doesn't convert online. Recommend either cutting its budget by half and reallocating, or confirming the ordering funnel works before continuing to fund it.

MEDIUM

PMax_Cube_Top Locations ramped to $214 spend this period (from ~$0) and returned just $59 in online orders (True ROAS 0.28, 3 orders). It overlaps geographically with the per-store PMax campaigns, so it is both the least efficient campaign and actively cannibalizing the location campaigns' impressions and data. Recommend pausing it and letting that budget flow back to the store-level campaigns — this was flagged last week when it was still spinning up, and the data now confirms it.
